# Compliance and Legal Boundary

WorkBuddy stores immutable versions of commercial and compliance documents and records tenant acceptance against the exact SHA-256 content hash.

Required GA document keys:

- `terms`;
- `privacy`;
- `dpa`;
- `subprocessors`;
- `security_whitepaper`.

The templates included in `docs/legal_templates` are drafting checklists only. They are not legal advice, are not approved for any jurisdiction and must not be published without qualified review.

## Required organizational work

- define controller/processor roles;
- establish data retention and deletion periods;
- document model and cloud subprocessors;
- complete transfer and regional-processing analysis;
- define customer notice and consent obligations;
- review AI-generated content and professional-services limitations;
- establish security incident notification commitments;
- determine tax, invoicing and consumer/business sales obligations.
