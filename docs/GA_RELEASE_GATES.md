# Commercial GA Release Gates

## Gate Commercial

Evidence:

- billing dry run;
- onboarding rehearsal;
- support SLA drill;
- legal documents published;
- tenant exit drill.

Required roles: Product, Finance, Operations and Privacy owners.

## Gate Value

Evidence:

- Design Partner results;
- weekly active rate;
- artifact adoption rate;
- measured time saved;
- pilot conversion rate;
- unit economics.

Required roles: Product, Business and Finance owners.

## Gate GA

Evidence:

- Production Open GO;
- current penetration test;
- privacy/legal approval;
- 30 days without P0/P1;
- support and On-call readiness;
- verified customer exit.

Required roles: Product, Platform, Security, Privacy, Operations and Finance owners.

## Automatic blockers

GA remains `NO_GO` when:

- linked Production Pilot is not GO;
- no active trial/paid subscription exists;
- no customer onboarding reached Completed;
- a P0/P1 or major incident remains open;
- required commercial/compliance documents are not published;
- evidence or accountable signatures are missing.

A signature is valid only for the current verified evidence snapshot. Adding or replacing evidence invalidates prior signatures.
