# Terms of Service Drafting Checklist — Not Approved Legal Text

- contracting entity and customer entity;
- service description and AI limitations;
- customer administrator responsibilities;
- approval and external-action boundaries;
- acceptable use;
- fees, taxes, renewal and suspension;
- confidentiality and intellectual property;
- data processing references;
- warranty disclaimer and liability allocation;
- support, service levels and change management;
- termination, export and deletion;
- governing law and dispute process.
